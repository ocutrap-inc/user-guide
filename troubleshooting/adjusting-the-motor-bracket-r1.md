# Adjusting the motor bracket (R1 only)

Move the motor bracket toward the trap's **front opening** to reduce the gap and help improve catches.

**Tools:** two nut drivers that fit the mounting hardware.

**Before starting:** fully lower the door and unplug the motor.

## 1. Remove the black top motor bracket

Support the motor and bracket. Remove the black bracket's **two bolts and washers**, then lift it off the metal plate. Save the hardware for reassembly. Leave both metal brackets on the cage.

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/r1-top-motor-bracket-assembly-cropped.png" alt="Black top motor bracket with its two removable bolts and washers" width="480" style="width: 480px; max-width: 100%; height: auto;">

## 2. Identify the metal brackets

The assembly has a **top metal bracket** (shown below) and an **inner metal bracket**, secured by **six bolts with nuts underneath**.

The inner bracket's **flat side sits on top of the cage**; its **two press-fit inserts protrude inside the trap**. Maintain this orientation.

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/r1-motor-bracket-location.png" alt="Top metal bracket on the OcuTrap R1, with the black bracket removed" width="480" style="width: 480px; max-width: 100%; height: auto;">

## 3. Loosen the six mounting nuts

Fit one driver onto the nut underneath and the other onto the bolt head above. **Hold the lower driver steady; turn the upper driver counterclockwise**, viewed from above.

Repeat at all six bolts, loosening just enough for the assembly to slide. **Keep the nuts and bolts installed.**

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/r1-motor-bracket-hold-and-turn.png" alt="Turn the upper nut driver while holding the lower driver steady" width="480" style="width: 480px; max-width: 100%; height: auto;">

## 4. Slide toward the front opening

Hold both ends of the metal plate and slide it **toward the front opening**. Usually, only a small movement is needed.

<img src="../.gitbook/assets/r1-motor-bracket-slide.png" alt="Sliding adjustment of the R1 motor mounting plate" width="480" style="width: 480px; max-width: 100%; height: auto;">

## 5. Tighten all six bolts

Hold the plate in position. Hold the lower driver steady and turn the upper driver clockwise. Tighten all six bolts and check that the plate cannot slide.

## 6. Reinstall the black top motor bracket

Place the black bracket on the plate and securely reinstall its two bolts and washers. See [R1 hardware setup](../getting-started/setting-up.md#step-3-setup-the-motor) if needed.

Clear your hands and tools, reconnect the motor, and check that the door opens and closes smoothly. It must sit **behind and underneath the locking bar across its full width** ([see locking check](door-wont-lock.md)).

Before readjusting, fully lower the door and unplug the motor. Contact [support](../support/support.md) if it still will not close and lock correctly.
