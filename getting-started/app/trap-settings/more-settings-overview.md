# Settings Overview

Open a trap and select the **Settings** tab to adjust how it detects, captures, and alerts. Everything is on one screen — there's no separate "More Settings" or "Advanced Settings" page. For exact ranges and defaults, see the [Settings Reference](settings-reference.md).

> **Note:** After changing settings, a reboot of the trap is recommended so all changes take effect.

## Battery

* **Battery Type** — tell the trap which battery is installed so level estimates and alerts are accurate.
* **Low / Critical battery threshold** — the battery levels (as percentages) at which you're warned.

## Weather

* **Weather Alerts** — get notified when the outdoor temperature at the trap crosses limits you set.

## General

* **Enable Beeps** — audible feedback from the trap.
* **Use Metric Units** — show measurements in metric.
* **Enable GPS** — let the trap report its location.

## Camera

* **Image Size** — the resolution of captured photos.
* **Image Rotation** — rotate photos if the trap is mounted sideways or upside down.
* **Time-lapse Interval** — take periodic photos while armed.

## Scout

* **Scout Image Quality** and **Scout Photo Interval** — resolution and frequency of photos while the trap is in Scout mode. See [Scouting Mode](../scouting-mode.md).

## Image and detection

* **Image Cropping** — trim any edge out of the photo (e.g. a bright cage bar).
* **Capture Distance** — how far inside the trap an animal must be before the door closes.
* **Accelerometer Time**, **Dark Threshold**, and **Min / Max IR Brightness** — fine-tune motion sensitivity and night-vision lighting.

## Alerts

* **Capture Alert Mode** — when to send capture alerts.
* **Pre-Capture Alerts** — alert when an animal enters the detection area, before a capture.
* **Battery Alerts** — send low- and critical-battery notifications.

## Advanced power

* **Battery Thresholds** and **Power Off Voltage** — the voltages behind the battery percentages; most users won't need these.
* **Arm When Offline** — let the trap arm even when it can't reach the network.
