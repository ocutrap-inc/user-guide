# Quick task q25 — Document in-app ownership transfer

**Date:** 2026-07-15
**Spec:** REQ-TRAP-TRANSFER-01 (app/requirements.md §7.17)
**exit-impact:** platform

## Problem

The app gained an in-app "Transfer ownership" action (trap Settings → Danger
zone, owner-only, history-carrying) alongside the existing remove-then-re-add
resale path. The guide's `selling-or-transferring-a-trap.md` documents only
the remove-then-re-add path and asserts "they do not transfer to the new
owner" about images, which stops being the whole story once transfer ships.
Customer-visible change ⇒ user-guide pass (agents.md §4).

## Plan

1. Restructure `device-management/selling-or-transferring-a-trap.md` into two
   options: **Transfer ownership in the app** (keeps capture history; new
   owner needs an existing account; subscription must be canceled first,
   org-billed traps leave org billing first) and **Remove, then re-add**
   (fresh start, existing content). Lead with the history distinction so the
   privacy consequence is the choosing criterion.
2. Do NOT document third-party share behavior after transfer — that ruling is
   pending product-side; the page stays silent on it rather than asserting
   something that may change.
3. Mirror to `docs-site/content/…`, regenerate the KB PDF
   (`scripts/build_kb_pdf.py`), verify, PR.
4. **Merge gate:** the PR merges only after the transfer UI is live on
   app.ocutrap.com (feature merged to app main but not yet prod-deployed at
   drafting time). Docs follow product.
