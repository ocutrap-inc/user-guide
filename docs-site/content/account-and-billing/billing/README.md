# Billing

## 1. Signing In

1. **Log in** to your OcuTrap account at [app.ocutrap.com](https://app.ocutrap.com).
2. The sidebar has **Settings**, **Billing**, and **Account**. **Account** holds your personal information and security settings. **Billing** holds your subscriptions.

The **Billing** page shows your plan, your **Payment method**, **Per-trap status** for every trap you can see, and **Invoices**. Each trap has its own subscription, so select **Manage** next to a trap to open its **Trap billing** page.

## 2. Opening the Billing Portal

1. On the **Billing** page, select **Payment portal** at the top, or **Manage in portal** on the **Payment method** card.
2. On a single trap’s **Trap billing** page, select **Manage subscription** (or **Update payment** / **Resolve payment** if a payment failed).

<!-- TODO: screenshot: Billing page header with the Payment portal button -->

In the portal you can:

* **Update billing information** (address, payment method, etc.)
* **Manage credit cards** (add/remove payment methods)
* **Cancel a subscription**
* **View past invoices**

Switching between **Monthly** and **Annual** is done in the app, not the portal. See section 3.2.

***

### 3. Managing Your Subscriptions

Once you are in the secure billing portal, you’ll see an overview of your subscriptions for your traps. From here, you can:

#### 3.1 Update Billing Information

* **Address:** Update your physical billing or shipping address.
* **Credit Cards:** Add, remove, or modify your credit card on file.
* **Payment Methods:** Choose a default payment method if you have multiple.

#### 3.2 Change Subscription Plans

There is one plan, the **Cellular plan**, billed **Monthly** or **Annual** per trap. There are no feature tiers.

* **Switch billing interval:** Do this in the app, not the portal. On the trap’s **Trap billing** page, under **Cellular plan**, select **Monthly** or **Annual**.
* **Cancel:** End the subscription in the Stripe portal. You’ll still have access for the remainder of your billing cycle.

#### 3.3 Status Definitions

These are the statuses shown on the **Billing** page under **Per-trap status**:

* **Active**\
  The subscription is paid and the trap has full function.
* **Trial**\
  The trap is in its free trial. You aren’t charged until the trial ends.
* **Past Due**\
  A payment failed. The trap still works for now. Select **Update payment**.
* **Suspended**\
  Payment stayed unresolved. The trap stops taking images and accepting commands. Select **Resolve payment**.
* **Canceled**\
  The subscription was canceled. Access continues until the cancellation date takes effect.
* **Paused**\
  You paused a monthly plan. Billing is stopped and the trap is dormant.
* **Inactive**\
  No subscription was ever started for this trap.

If you have questions about a status, [contact our support team](https://ocutrap.com/pages/contact).

### 4. Frequently Asked Questions

#### 4.1 How do I update my payment method?

1. On the **Billing** page, select **Payment portal** (or **Manage in portal** on the **Payment method** card).
2. Select **Payment Methods**.
3. Add a new card or edit existing card details.
4. Confirm your changes.

Full steps: [Changing Your Subscription Payment Method](changing-your-subscription-payment-method.md).

#### 4.2 Can I view my billing history?

Yes. The **Billing** page has an **Invoices** section covering all traps, with **View** and **Receipt** on each row. A single trap’s **Trap billing** page has **Recent invoices** for that trap only.
