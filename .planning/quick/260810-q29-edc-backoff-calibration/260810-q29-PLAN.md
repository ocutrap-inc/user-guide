# Quick task q29 — EDC backoff calibration

**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Spec:** DOC-19
**exit-impact:** ops

## Problem

The guide names the EDC Backoff setting but does not give a complete field
calibration procedure. A customer can tell that lower and higher values change
the reverse movement, but cannot tell how to choose a value, how battery charge
affects the test, or how both an under-retract and an over-retract can leave the
door on top of the fixed locking bar.

## Plan

1. Expand the canonical Enhanced Door Closing page with the fixed-bar
   mechanism, the EDC Backoff setting, charged-battery calibration in 10 ms
   steps, success criteria, and an empty-trap recovery test.
2. Make the recovery test cautious and repeatable: no animal, hands clear, a
   long wooden handle used from outside the cage, and the obstruction removed
   before the re-seat.
3. Link both ways to the app Open/Close control and the physical-button door
   sequence. Document both current setting locations without claiming that EDC
   verifies the lock.
4. Tighten the setting summary and reference-table row, then carry the same
   field procedure into the hand-maintained Knowledge Base source.
5. Regenerate the `docs-site/content/` mirrors and Knowledge Base PDF. Verify
   mirrors, links/build, PDF source hash, and rendered PDF text.

## Out of scope

- Changing firmware or app behavior.
- Selecting one fleet-wide timing value. EDC backoff is calibrated per trap.
- Claiming automatic lock detection; the R1 has no lock-position sensor.
