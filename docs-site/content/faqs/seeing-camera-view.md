# Seeing Camera view

To open the camera view, click the downward arrow icon located beneath the settings button.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/drop down image.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The camera view shows the most recent image from inside your trap. From here you can:

* **Request a fresh photo** — a fast lower-quality image or a full-quality one; see [Manually taking an image](manually-taking-an-image.md).
* **Browse past images** — open the gallery from the top-left grid icon.

If images look too dark, washed out, or blurry, see [Camera](camera.md) for the light-switching settings and fixes.
