# Resetting Password

If you forget your password, you can reset it with a code emailed to you:

1. Go to [app.ocutrap.com](https://app.ocutrap.com) and click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email and click **Send Reset Code**.
3. Check your email for a **6-digit reset code**.
4. Back in the app, click **Enter Reset Code**, then enter the code along with your **new password** and confirm it.
5. Click **Reset Password**. When you see **Password reset complete**, sign in with your new password.

Your password is kept in sync across the web and mobile apps, so the new password works everywhere.
