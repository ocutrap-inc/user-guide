# Resetting the Mobile App

If the OcuTrap app is frozen, won't load, or you're not getting push notifications, a full quit or reinstall usually fixes it. Your **account, traps, images, and subscription stay on the server** — resetting the app on your phone does not delete them. After you reinstall, sign in with the same email and password.

Try the steps below in order. Start with a force quit; only reinstall if that doesn't help.

***

## Force Quit the App

Closing the app from the home screen isn't always enough. Use the app switcher so it fully stops, then open it again.

### iPhone / iPad (iOS)

1. Open the **App Switcher**: swipe up from the bottom of the screen and pause in the middle (or double-click the Home button on older devices).
2. Find the **OcuTrap** card and swipe it **up** to close it.
3. Go back to the Home Screen and open **OcuTrap** again.

### Android

1. Open the **Recents** / app switcher (usually the square or recent-apps button, or a swipe-up gesture — depends on your phone).
2. Find **OcuTrap** and swipe it away (or use **Close all** if your phone offers that).
3. Open **OcuTrap** again from your Home Screen or app drawer.

***

## Clear Cache (Android only)

On Android you can clear temporary files without uninstalling. This does **not** remove your signed-in session on every phone, but if the app still misbehaves after force quit, try it before a full reinstall.

1. Open your phone's **Settings**.
2. Tap **Apps** (or **Apps & notifications**), then **OcuTrap**.
3. Tap **Storage** (or **Storage & cache**).
4. Tap **Clear cache** — prefer this over **Clear storage** / **Clear data**, which signs you out and wipes local app data.

iPhone does not expose a separate "clear cache" control for apps. If force quit isn't enough on iOS, go to delete and reinstall below.

***

## Delete and Reinstall the App

Use this when force quit (and Android cache clear) didn't fix the problem, or when support asks you to reinstall so the app can re-register for push notifications.

{% hint style="info" %}
Before you delete the app, make sure you know the **email and password** for your OcuTrap account. You'll need them to sign in again. If you forgot your password, reset it first — see [Can't Sign In to Your Account](cant-sign-in.md).
{% endhint %}

### iPhone / iPad (iOS)

1. Touch and hold the **OcuTrap** icon on the Home Screen.
2. Tap **Remove App**, then **Delete App**, and confirm.
3. Open the **App Store**, search for **OcuTrap**, and tap **Get** / the cloud icon to install again.
   * Direct link: [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1539244938)
4. Open **OcuTrap** and sign in with your email and password.
5. When prompted, **Allow Notifications** so push alerts work again.
   * You can also turn them on later: **Settings → Notifications → OcuTrap → Allow Notifications**, then confirm **Push Notifications** are on in the app's **Notification Settings**.

### Android

1. Open **Settings → Apps → OcuTrap**, then tap **Uninstall** (or touch and hold the OcuTrap icon and choose **Uninstall**).
2. Open **Google Play**, search for **OcuTrap**, and tap **Install**.
   * Direct link: [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ocutrap.ocutrap)
3. Open **OcuTrap** and sign in with your email and password.
4. When prompted, allow notifications for OcuTrap.
   * You can also turn them on later: **Settings → Apps → OcuTrap → Notifications**, then confirm **Push Notifications** are on in the app's **Notification Settings**.

***

## After You Reinstall

1. Sign in with the **same account** you used before — your traps should appear as usual.
2. Open **Notification Settings** in the app and turn on **Push Notifications** plus the alert types you want.
3. Confirm the phone itself still allows OcuTrap notifications (steps above).

## What This Does Not Change

* Your **OcuTrap account**, password, and email stay the same.
* Your **traps**, capture history, and images stay on your account.
* Your **subscription and billing** are unchanged.
* This is not the same as [deleting your account](../account-and-billing/account-deletion.md) or [resetting your password](../account-and-billing/resetting-password.md).

* What you already tried (force quit, clear cache, reinstall)
* A short description of what the app is doing wrong (a screenshot helps)
