# Trap Settings

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (30).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Trap settings control how each trap detects, captures, and alerts — including your **capture distance**, **notification preferences**, and **camera behavior**. Open a trap and tap **Settings** to adjust them; tap **More Settings** for the full list.

## General

The top of **Settings** covers how you identify the trap. None of it changes how the trap behaves.

* **Name** — what the trap is called in your lists and alerts.
* **Location note** — a short line about where the trap sits, like "Back fence line, under the cedar".
* **Notes** — a longer, free-form note for anything else you need on site: the customer the trap is placed with, access directions, a gate code. It appears on the trap's **Overview**, on both the web app and your phone.

Everyone you share the trap with can read these, so leave out anything you would not want a shared user to see. The trap owner and managers can edit them.
