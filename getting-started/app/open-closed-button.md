# Open / Close Door

_Open a trap from your Traps list to reach its detail page, where the **Controls** card lives._

### Open / Close door

The Controls card has one door button that toggles between the two states:

* **Open door**: Lifts the door for resetting the trap or letting an animal exit.
* **Close door**: Shuts and locks the door, preventing escape or readying the trap for transport.
* The button label and the trap's status always show the latest reported door state.

> **Releasing a captured animal:** while a trap is **Captured**, the door button is replaced by **Disarm & release**. Tap it to open the door, free the animal, and disarm the trap in one step (the app asks you to confirm first). The trap never auto-releases — see [After a Capture](after-capture.md).

> **Before Scouting Mode:** the door must be fully open. See [Scouting Mode](scouting-mode.md) for how scouting works without closing the door.
