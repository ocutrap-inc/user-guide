### Quick Start Guide

The booklet that ships inside every trap. The print-ready files are two letter sheets, already laid out for folding: print double-sided, flip on the short edge, one page per sheet, fold each sheet in half along the center line. For the R1, nest sheet 2 inside sheet 1.
