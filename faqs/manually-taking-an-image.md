# Manually taking an image

You can request a fresh photo of the inside of your trap at any time. Open the trap's detail screen — the most recent photo is shown as a large image (the camera view) with the trap's controls beside it.

There are two ways to ask the trap for a new photo:

* **Request image** — a standard, full-quality photo. This is the best choice for a clear look inside the trap. See [Taking higher quality images](taking-higher-quality-images.md).
* **Request quick image** — a faster, lower-quality photo. Use this when you just need to check on something right now and don't need full detail.

After you request a photo, an **Image incoming…** indicator appears on the camera view while the trap captures and uploads the new image. It clears on its own once the fresh photo arrives.
