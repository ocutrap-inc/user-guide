# Bug reporting

If you find a bug, please report it by emailing [support@ocutrap.com](mailto:support@ocutrap.com?subject=Bug%20Report%20from%20Knowledge%20Base). Include a detailed bug description, reproduction steps, and any relevant visuals. Explain what you expected to happen and what actually did. Your detailed feedback helps improve our service.

You can also reach us through the chat bubble on the [OcuTrap.com](https://www.ocutrap.com) homepage.
