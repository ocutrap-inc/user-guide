# Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your OcuTrap account from the web app or the mobile app. Deletion cannot be undone.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Warning:** Deleting your account permanently deletes your owned traps and their data, removes sharing access, and removes you from organizations (where allowed). It cannot be undone.
{% endhint %}

## Before you start

1. **Cancel active trap subscriptions** in Billing. The app blocks account deletion while you still own an active or trialing subscription.
2. If you are the **only owner** (or the billing owner) of an organization, **transfer ownership or delete that organization** first. The app will block deletion until those orgs are resolved.

## What deletion does

When you confirm deletion, OcuTrap will:

- Permanently delete **traps you own**, including captures/images, settings, vitals, and location history for those traps
- **Remove sharing access** — revoke shares you granted to others, and remove you from traps that were shared with you
- Remove your **organization memberships** (after the owner/billing guards above are satisfied)
- Permanently delete your **account** and sign you out

It does **not** cancel Stripe subscriptions for you — cancel those in Billing first.

## Web

1. Sign in at [app.ocutrap.com](https://app.ocutrap.com) and open **Account**.
2. Scroll to the **Danger zone** section and, next to **Delete account**, click **Delete**.
3. Read the consequences list in the confirmation dialog.
4. **Re-enter your account email** to confirm (capitalization does not matter).
5. Click **Delete account**.

You are signed out when deletion completes.

## Mobile

1. Open the OcuTrap mobile app and go to **Settings → Account**.
2. Under **Danger zone**, tap **Delete account**.
3. Read the consequences list on the confirmation screen.
4. **Type your account email** to confirm (capitalization does not matter).
5. Tap **Delete account**.

You are signed out when deletion completes.
