# Taking higher quality images

For the clearest possible view inside your trap, request a standard **full-quality** image instead of a quick one.

On the trap's detail screen, use **Request image**. The trap captures a full-quality photo and sends it to the app. This takes a little longer than a [quick image](manually-taking-an-image.md), but the detail is much better — useful for confirming what's inside the trap or checking image clarity.
