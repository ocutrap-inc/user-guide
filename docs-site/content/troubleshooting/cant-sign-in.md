# Can't sign in to your account

Sign-in problems almost always come down to one of a few causes. Find the section below that matches what you see. Your email address is your username, and the same email and password work on the web app at [app.ocutrap.com](https://app.ocutrap.com) and in the mobile app.

## Forgot Your Password

Reset it from the email we send you:

1. Go to [app.ocutrap.com](https://app.ocutrap.com) and click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email and click **Send Reset Code**.
3. Open the email and click **Reset Password**. The page opens headed **Choose a new password**.
4. Enter your new password, confirm it, and submit. When you see **Password reset complete**, sign in with your new password.

If your mail app strips the button, the email also carries a reset code. Click **Enter Reset Code** in the app and paste it in. The code is **long** (32 characters of mixed letters and numbers) and **case-sensitive**, so paste it rather than typing it.

Your new password needs at least 10 characters. No required mix of numbers or symbols. Common or breached passwords are rejected.

## Reset Code Didn't Arrive

Codes usually land within a minute or two. If yours has not:

1. Check your **spam or junk folder**, and search your inbox for "OcuTrap".
2. Confirm you entered the **email you registered with**. Codes only send to addresses that have an OcuTrap account. If the app says "Unable to send reset email", the address you entered likely does not match an account.
3. On the reset screen, click **Didn't receive a code? Send again**, then use the newest code from your inbox.
4. On a work email? Company mail filters sometimes hold automated messages. Ask your IT team to allow mail from OcuTrap.

Still nothing after a few tries? Email [support@ocutrap.com](mailto:support@ocutrap.com) and we can help from our side.

## Sign-In Isn't Working

Match what you see on the sign-in page:

* **"The email or password you entered is incorrect."** Check for typos and caps lock. Click the eye icon in the password field to see what you typed. If it still fails, reset your password using the steps above.
* **"Too many attempts. Please wait a moment and try again."** Sign-in is briefly limited after repeated failures. Wait a few minutes, then try once.
* **A verification code screen appears.** Some accounts confirm their email before signing in. Enter the code we emailed you and click **Verify and continue**.
* **The form asks for your first and last name.** That is the sign-up form. Click **Sign in instead** at the bottom to get back to sign-in.
* **You deleted your account.** Account deletion is permanent and the old sign-in will not work. You would need to create a new account.

Passwords stay in sync between the web and mobile apps, so a password that works on one works on the other.

## Lost Access to Your Email

Your email address is your OcuTrap username, so we verify it is really you before changing anything.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Do not create a second account.** Your traps, images, and subscription are attached to your original email.
{% endhint %}

Email [support@ocutrap.com](mailto:support@ocutrap.com) from any address you can use. Include the email on your account, your **Trap ID**(s), and your name so we can verify ownership and help you regain access.

## Coming From the Old OcuTrap App

The old app at `base.ocutrap.com` and the new app at [app.ocutrap.com](https://app.ocutrap.com) use separate sign-ins. Your old password does not work on the new app. Full walkthrough: [Move to the new OcuTrap app](../switching-to-the-new-app/README.md).

* Your account already exists in the new app under the **same email** you used before. Do not sign up for a new one.
* When it is your turn to move, you get an email from OcuTrap with a **Set your password** link. Open it, choose a new password, and sign in at [app.ocutrap.com](https://app.ocutrap.com).
* No email yet? The move happens in waves. Keep using the old app until yours arrives.
* Lost the email? Contact [support@ocutrap.com](mailto:support@ocutrap.com) and we will send a fresh link.

## Still Stuck

Email [support@ocutrap.com](mailto:support@ocutrap.com) with:

* The **email address** on your account
* The **exact error message** you see (a screenshot helps)
* Whether you were on the **web app** or the **mobile app**
