- Approach photos (ARMED): first on entry + up to 2-photo burst; then on ~3 in (75 mm) more inward progress, else every 10 min if stalled; fast/grayscale.
- ARMED presence + SCOUT photos: **~every 15 s** while in-zone (scout interval settable 15 s–5 min, 0 = off). **No 5-second cadence exists** — prior docs were wrong.
- Scout Alert/Trigger: one photo each; alert text throttled 5 min, photos still flow.
- Post-capture: ~every 15 s burst for 1 min, then a check-in photo every 2 h.
- Time-lapse: default 6 h (0–24 h; 0 = off). Pre-capture alert text throttled 60 s (photos not).
- Scheduled photos pause off normal power and while the camera is busy.
