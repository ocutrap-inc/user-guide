# Resetting Password

If you forget your password, you can reset it from the email we send you:

1. Go to [app.ocutrap.com](https://app.ocutrap.com) and click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email and click **Send Reset Code**.
3. Open the email and click **Reset Password**. The page opens headed **Choose a new password**, already knowing your email and code.
4. Enter your **new password**, confirm it, and submit. When you see **Password reset complete**, sign in with your new password.

### If the Button Doesn't Work

Some mail apps strip buttons out. The email carries a reset code underneath as a backup:

1. Back in the app, click **Enter Reset Code**.
2. Copy the code from the email and paste it in, then enter your new password.

The code is **long** (32 characters of mixed letters and numbers), so copy and paste it rather than typing it. It is also **case-sensitive**. If your phone capitalizes the first character for you, the code stops working, so check the first letter after pasting.

Codes are single use. If one is rejected, click **Didn't receive a code? Send again** and use the newest email.
