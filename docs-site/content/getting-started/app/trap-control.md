# Trap Control

Every command for a trap lives in one place: the **Controls** card on the trap's detail page. Open a trap from your Traps list to get there — nothing is hidden in a menu.

Above the Controls card, a **vitals strip** shows the trap's battery, signal, and when it was last heard from. Select **Last heard** to **Ping** the trap — asking it to check in right now.

## Set the mode

At the top of the Controls card, the **arm-mode dropdown** sets what the trap does. Each option explains itself, so the difference is clear before you choose:

* **Off** — the trap stands down. It won't catch or send alerts.
* **Scout** — the trap watches and sends photos but never closes the door. See [Scouting Mode](scouting-mode.md).
* **Armed** — the trap catches and holds. See [Arm / Unarm](arm-un-arm-button.md).

Picking the mode the trap is already in does nothing. The trap never releases on its own — you do that here.

## Door

* **Open door / Close door** — one button toggles the door to the opposite of its current state. See [Open / Closed](open-closed-button.md).
* **Disarm** — returns an Armed or Scout trap to Off.

When a trap is **Captured**, these are replaced by **Disarm & release** (opens the door and frees the animal) and **Snooze** (defers the next reminder). See [After a Capture](after-capture.md).

## Quick actions

A row of quick actions sits under the door controls:

* **Request image** — ask the camera for a fresh photo. See [Manually taking an image](../../faqs/manually-taking-an-image.md).
* **Location** — request an updated GPS fix for the trap. See [GPS](../../faqs/gps.md).
* **Buzz** — sound the trap's buzzer, handy for locating the trap or confirming it's responsive.

To **Ping** the trap (ask it to check in now), select **Last heard** in the vitals strip at the top of the page.

## Advanced

Open the **Advanced** section for commands you'll rarely need. Each asks you to confirm first:

* **Reboot** — restart the device. It's briefly offline while it restarts.
* **Sleep** — put the trap into low-power sleep until it's woken (press the power button or change the battery). See [Power Modes](../../faqs/power-modes.md).

## Device details and history

* **Trap info** (Overview tab) — temperature, firmware version, site, and the trap's IDs. Battery and signal are in the vitals strip above the Controls card.
* **History & diagnostics** (below the tabs) — charts of battery, signal, and temperature over time, plus GPS location history.
